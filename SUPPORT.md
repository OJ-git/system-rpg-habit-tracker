## Frequently asked questions

### Getting started

**What is Odyssey?**
Odyssey turns your habits, goals, and focus sessions into a role-playing game. Complete daily quests to earn XP and gold, level up your core stats, build long-term milestone journeys, and spend gold on real-world rewards you set for yourself.

**How do I create a quest?**
Tap the **+** button on the Quests screen, give your quest a name, pick the stat it builds, and save. Swipe **right** on a quest to complete it, or swipe **left** to edit it.

**What are the core stats?**
Every quest builds one of four core stats — **Physique, Mind, Wealth, Bond** — plus up to four custom attributes you define yourself. Your progress shows on your radar.

**What are milestone journeys?**
A milestone journey turns a big long-term goal (like "run a marathon") into a step-by-step roadmap. Each attribute can have its own journey so small wins add up over time.

**How do focus sessions work?**
Stake your daily quests on a focus timer. Stay in the app and finish the session to earn bonus XP and gold. Leave the app early and you lose the run.

**How do real-world rewards work?**
Save the gold you earn and spend it on real-life treats you set for yourself — a coffee, a movie, an afternoon off. Rest becomes something you earned.

### Subscriptions & purchases

**How do I manage or cancel my Odyssey Pro subscription?**
Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID, not inside Odyssey. On your iPhone open **Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions**, select Odyssey, and change or cancel your plan there. Cancelling keeps Pro active until the end of the current billing period.

**How do I restore a purchase?**
If you reinstalled the app or switched devices, open Odyssey's settings and tap **Restore Purchases**, or reinstall the app while signed in to the same Apple ID.

**I was charged but Pro didn't unlock — what do I do?**
Try **Restore Purchases** first. If that doesn't work, email us at [olzhasb1406@gmail.com](mailto:olzhasb1406@gmail.com) with the date of the charge and we'll help sort it out. Refund requests are handled by Apple at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

### Data & privacy

**Where is my data stored?**
Almost everything stays on your device using Apple's on-device storage. If you enable iCloud sync, your data syncs privately through your own iCloud account — we never see it. Screen Time data never leaves your device.

**How do I delete my data?**
You can delete your account and all related data at any time from inside the app's settings. For full details, see our [Privacy Policy](https://oj-git.github.io/system-rpg-habit-tracker/PRIVACY.html).

**Does Odyssey use ads or third-party trackers?**
No. There are no ads and no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

### Troubleshooting

**The app is behaving unexpectedly.**
First, make sure you're on the latest version from the App Store and that your device is running a recent version of iOS. Restarting the app usually resolves temporary glitches. If a problem persists, email us with the details above and we'll investigate.
